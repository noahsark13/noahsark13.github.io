DIRECT3D 12 RENDERER

Team

Timeline

Stack
C++ · HLSL · Direct3D 12 · DXR

This C++ renderer comes from two graphics programming classes. I built the Direct3D 11 version in the first one, then ported it to low-level Direct3D 12 in the second, one assignment at a time. Each assignment set a goal, and I built it from what we covered in lecture and the course’s demo code.

  • Raster renderer — bindless textures, and the PBR shader ported from the Direct3D 11 version
  • Particles — fire, smoke, sparks and snow, with the fire layered from three emitters
  • DXR path tracer — 10 paths per pixel with up to 15 bounces, and a shadow ray toward the sun at every hit
