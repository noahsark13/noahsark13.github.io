Changeling VR is a faculty-led VR mystery game released on Steam, built by several teams working in parallel. Over a summer co-op on its 10-person tech art team, I modeled and textured VR-optimized, game-ready assets and materials for Unreal Engine 4 across several of its levels. Other teams depended on those assets to keep their levels moving, so I worked to their deadlines.

  • Subway cars — an R160-based redesign, with a dummy car and a full interior
  • Toy gear material — glitter in the plastic, with color and pattern swaps
  • Street & home — the bodega, street pieces and a stainless steel stove

Takeaways

  • This was the biggest team I’d worked on. Daily stand-ups across the aesthetics branch kept everyone aware of what the other teams were doing, so the work never felt isolated.
  • The onboarding greenhouse was redone several times because the reference the level team had agreed on never reached the art team. I learned to confirm direction early, to take big design changes to the game director first, and to show work in progress sooner instead of near the end.
