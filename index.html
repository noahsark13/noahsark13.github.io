ABOUT
ME.

Email noahkasper9@gmail.com LinkedIn linkedin.com/in/noahkasper (opens in a new tab) Resume PDF — 1 page (opens in a new tab)

I’ve always felt a connection to games. Ever since I was a kid, I’ve been making them, from hand-drawn board games to simple paper cutouts. I grew up surrounded by emerging technology. A Mac, a PlayStation and a Wii introduced me to video games at an early age, and eventually to Roblox, where the appeal of making my own games for free pulled me in.

Were my first games good? Not really, but that didn’t matter. They were cool to me, I loved showing them off, and I took it on myself to make the next one better. That drive to improve my craft pushed me from scripting into 3D modeling, texturing and finishing whole projects. Eventually I started picking up commission work on Roblox, at first for virtual currency or just for fun, and later for real money. Each job taught me more about working for others and to a standard.

That led me to RIT, where I put myself in a more structured environment to grow my skills and workflow. Over those four years, I made great connections and worked on games and projects that, even when they weren’t perfect, left me with lessons I bring to every project.
